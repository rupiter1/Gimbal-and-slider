# Gimbal-and-slider
a dev project to learn all about steper motors and make a fun and very useful camera slider and gimbal for my sony a6400 and all other camera's using a 1/4" mount

intro, 
a dev project to learn all about steper motors and make a fun and very useful camera slider and gimbal for my sony a6400 and all other camera's using a 1/4" mount. using nema 17 motors and adjustable axis this will proform well under the load it's build for which is an a6400 and the tamron 17-70 f2.8 lens. a total weight of 928grams. and the second project, the camera slider! a smooth way to record really smooth shoot moving from the sunset to the beatiful flower (macro shot) 

why make this your self.
im making an easy to follow list of steps to follow and parts to buy to where you can come into this project with some soldering skills and leave in a weekend with a working gimbal and maybe even slider!. this wont have the amazing face tracking of the dji gimbal or their smoothness but it will be truely yours without giving dji or anyone else the choice to brick your equiment with the push of a software update. not to meanstion that this will be much cheaper than an off the shelf dji roin gimbal.
(write good things first then why not good)
